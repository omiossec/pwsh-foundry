# Contoso Travel and Expense Policy

All business expenses are submitted through **Contoso ExpenseHub**. Paper claims and email submissions are not accepted.

## Approval thresholds

Approval requirements depend on the total value of the claim:

- Claims up to **25 EUR** may be submitted without a receipt.
- Claims above **25 EUR** require an itemised receipt attached to the claim.
- Claims above **75 EUR** require line manager approval.
- Claims above **500 EUR** require director sign-off in addition to line manager approval.
- Claims above **5,000 EUR** require Vice President approval and are reviewed by Finance before payment.

A claim may not be split into smaller claims to stay below a threshold. ExpenseHub flags claims from the same supplier on the same day and routes them for review as a single amount.

## Submission deadline

Expenses must be submitted within **21 calendar days** of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted after 21 days require a written justification and are approved at the discretion of the relevant director. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Per diem rates

When travelling on Contoso business, staff may claim a daily allowance instead of itemised meal receipts:

- Domestic travel: **62 EUR per day**
- International travel: **95 EUR per day**

The per diem covers all meals and incidental costs. Staff claiming a per diem may not additionally claim for individual meals on the same day.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at **0.38 EUR per kilometre**. The claim must record the start and end location of each journey. Commuting between home and the registered office is never reimbursable.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: traffic fines and parking penalties, in-flight upgrades purchased at the airport, personal entertainment, minibar charges, and loyalty programme fees. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client entertainment claim with the client's name recorded.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run. ExpenseHub closes for the month on the **20th at 17:00 CET**; claims approved after that cut-off are paid in the following month's run.
